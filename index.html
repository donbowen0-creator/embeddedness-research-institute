About

This website serves as the official research hub for the Community Embeddedness Model (CEM) and Recovery Embeddedness Model (REM).

Community Embeddedness Model

The Community Embeddedness Model proposes that long-term desistance from crime occurs through increasing integration into prosocial communities, institutions, and social relationships.

Recovery Embeddedness Model

The Recovery Embeddedness Model extends embeddedness theory to addiction recovery and examines how community support contributes to sustained recovery.
